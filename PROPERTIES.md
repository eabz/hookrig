# Properties

> Generated by the Fizz skill. Each property is described in English and has one
> of three states:
> - `[ ]` pending — not yet implemented; `/fizz-convert` will pick it up
> - `[x]` implemented — Solidity exists in Properties.sol / handlers
> - `[-]` skipped / manual — `/fizz-convert` will NOT touch it. Use this for TODO stubs,
>   properties that need human judgment, or properties you add yourself later.
>
> Users may freely add their own entries (use any unused `GL-NN` / `SP-NN` ID) and mark
> them `[ ]` to have `/fizz-convert` implement them, or `[-]` to leave them as a spec
> note only.
>
> IDs are stable. Do not renumber.
>
> **Guarantee tag.** Every property is tagged `SHOULD-HOLD` or `EXPLORATORY`:
> - `SHOULD-HOLD` — explicitly guaranteed by docs/spec/standard or an exact identity
>   (evidence cited inline). If the campaign violates it, that is a **confirmed bug**.
> - `EXPLORATORY` — inferred, not explicitly promised. A violation is a **lead flagged
>   for human review**, not an automatic bug.
>
> This tag is set at generation time so violations can be triaged without post-hoc
> severity guessing. If you edit a property's logic such that it is no longer guaranteed,
> change its tag to `EXPLORATORY`.
>
> Source IDs refer to the discovery reports in `fizz_data/discovery/` (CON = conservation,
> RT/RD = round trip and rounding, ST/VS/VT = state transitions, ADV = adversarial,
> SPEC = protocol-type templates). Several properties read internal storage through
> `HookRigOrchestratorHarness` (see `fizz_data/property-plan.md`, "Harness additions").

## Specific Properties
> Operation-gated postconditions. Implemented as `internal function property_*()` in `Properties.sol` and called from the relevant handler after `snapshotAfter()`.

- [x] **SP-01** — withdrawClaim is exact, isolated and always available. After `withdrawClaim(p, c, amt, to)` by claimant `X`: `poolClaims[p][c][X]` falls by exactly `amt`, the hook's ERC-6909 balance of `c` falls by exactly `amt`, `to` receives exactly `amt`, and every other `(pool, currency, account)` entry is unchanged. A claimant withdrawing their full balance never reverts. (Category: STATE_TRANSITION; Guarantee: SHOULD-HOLD — docs/06 AV-01 / docs/02 4.2 "claims double-keyed per pool"; burn(amount) then take(amount) in `HookRigBase.unlockCallback`; docs/04 §5 #2; After: `hookRigOrchestrator_withdrawClaim_clamped`, `hookRigOrchestrator_withdrawClaim_full`, `hookRigOrchestrator_withdrawClaim`; Priority: HIGH; Sources: CON-04, ST-03, RT-10, ADV-03)
- [x] **SP-02** — King payout is exact, single-currency, winner-only and live. A successful `claimKingPot(p, r, to)` comes from the round's recorded winner, lowers the hook's ERC-6909 balance by exactly the settled amount in the round's single funding currency (no other currency moves), pays `to` exactly that amount, and never succeeds twice for the same round. The recorded winner of an unclaimed, non-empty settled round can always claim it. (Category: HIGH_LEVEL; Guarantee: SHOULD-HOLD — docs/06 FF-03 (single-currency pot, jackpot must not freeze); `validateAndMarkClaimed` reverts on a claimed round; After: `hookRigOrchestrator_claimKingPot_clamped`, new `hookRigOrchestrator_claimKingPot_asWinner`; Priority: HIGH; Sources: CON-05, ST-08, SPEC-19, ADV-04, ADV-05)
- [x] **SP-03** — Limit-order escrow lifecycle. After `placeLimitOrder`, the hook's balance of the input currency (ERC-20 or ETH) has risen by exactly `inputAmount` and the maker's has fallen by the same amount. After cancel or claim, the maker receives no more than they paid at placement, in the input currency. An honest maker's cancel of an unfilled order (or claim of a filled one) only reverts with `BucketAlreadyFilled` (or `BucketNotYetFilled`). Expected to fail on native orders (`LimitOrdersBlock.sol:63`). (Category: HIGH_LEVEL; Guarantee: SHOULD-HOLD — docs/04 §5 #4 "users can always cancel unfilled orders" and recover escrow; docs/06 FF-01; refund == deposit for an unexecuted order; After: `hookRigOrchestrator_placeLimitOrder*`, `hookRigOrchestrator_cancelLimitOrder*`, `hookRigOrchestrator_claimLimitOrder*`, new `hookRigOrchestrator_limitOrderRoundTrip`; Priority: HIGH; Sources: CON-10, RT-08, RT-09, ST-10 [MANDATORY], ADV-01, ADV-02)
- [x] **SP-04** — No profit from a pool round trip. On any live pool, an exact-input swap A → B followed by an exact-input swap of all B received back to A leaves the actor's wallet A balance, plus any `poolClaims` the actor gained in A during the trip, no higher than before, and B unchanged. Runs in honest, spoofed and dust modes. (Category: HIGH_LEVEL; Guarantee: SHOULD-HOLD — docs/04 §5 #3: no sequence of dust swaps can extract net positive value (`valueOut <= valueIn`); After: new `pool_swapRoundTrip`; Priority: HIGH; Sources: RT-06, ADV-14, SPEC-21)
- [x] **SP-05** — No profit from a curve round trip. On an ActiveBonding VirtualXYK or SteppedTranche curve, a buy of `q` followed by selling every token received returns no more than `q` quote (including inside the anti-snipe surcharge window), leaves the project balance unchanged, and never lowers `realQuoteRaised` (+ `totalSurchargeCollected` for Tranche). (Category: HIGH_LEVEL; Guarantee: SHOULD-HOLD — docs/04 §5 #3 (`valueOut <= valueIn` for bonding-curve buy/sell cycles); x-ray E-2; After: new `virtualXYKLaunch_buySellRoundTrip`, `steppedTrancheLaunch_buySellRoundTrip`; Priority: HIGH; Sources: RT-01, RT-04, ADV-16, SPEC-09)
- [x] **SP-06** — No profit from repeated dust cycles on curves (C2). Repeating N (1–20) cycles of a dust buy (1 to 1e12 wei) followed by selling everything bought, on either curve type, never raises the actor's quote balance and never lowers the curve's `realQuoteRaised`. (Category: HIGH_LEVEL; Guarantee: SHOULD-HOLD — docs/04 §5 #3 ("bonding curve buy/sell cycles"); After: new `virtualXYKLaunch_dustCycle`, `steppedTrancheLaunch_dustCycle`; Priority: HIGH; Sources: RT-03, RT-05 (C2, non-mergeable))
- [x] **SP-07** — Graduation never reverts. A buy on an ActiveBonding curve large enough to reach its target, from a funded and approved actor, succeeds and leaves the curve Graduated with an initialized pool. The only allowed reverts are the curve wallet-cap errors (`WalletCapExceededOnCurve` / `WalletCapExceededOnTranche`). (Category: STATE_TRANSITION; Guarantee: SHOULD-HOLD — docs/04 §5 #5: fuzz tests "proving that graduation never reverts, refunds excess final buy amounts accurately"; After: `virtualXYKLaunch_buy_toGraduation`, `steppedTrancheLaunch_buy_toGraduation`; Priority: HIGH; Sources: SPEC-07, ADV-18)
